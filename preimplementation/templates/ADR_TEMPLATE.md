## Problem and constraints

State the concrete decision, applicable authority, compatibility and resource constraints, and consequences of delaying it.

## Alternatives

Describe at least the selected candidate and a credible alternative. Include maintenance, portability, isolation, failure behaviour and migration implications where relevant.

## Evidence and experiment

Specify fixtures, independent expected observations, thresholds set before measurement, environment and budget. Identify stopping conditions, failure evidence and result uncertainty. No result is implied by this template.

## Decision and rationale

Record the decision only after the authorised role makes it. Link evidence, limitations and dissent. Distinguish measured findings from assumptions.

## Implementation and migration

Identify work items, compatibility impacts, rollout, rollback or forward recovery, documentation changes and mandatory regression cases.

## Revisit triggers

Identify the version, workload, security or operational changes that invalidate the evidence and require reassessment.
